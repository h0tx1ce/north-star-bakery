Freshly Baked for Our Community

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery offering fresh breads, pastries, and cakes made with care. We welcome local families, commuters, and event planners looking for handmade baked goods.

What We're Known For

We are known for artisan breads, flaky pastries, celebration cakes, and friendly service. Our products are prepared in small batches with carefully selected ingredients.
